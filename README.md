# SoftwareLoveTracker

Tracking the support for minority demographics that technology corporations and organizations express, all in good faith.

## Disclaimers

This list does not track any perceived "favourtism" to the aforementioned, and any other demographic groups. It is designed solely in good faith, and not to direct hate towards identity groups. It serves to inform users of the background behind the softwares they may utilize in their lives, only in regards to support of the LGBTQ+ community and other minority demographics.

Additionally, just because a software is not on a list, does not mean it is against or for any ideology (Example: A software not being on Software for Diversity does not mean it is against such). There is simply not specific evidence that can be attributed to them, or the software has simply not been addressed in the list at all.

And finally, all of this list is done in good faith. None of this is intended to express any bias, bigotry, or discrimination towards anyone, and if you see something that might look like it, it is simply an honest mistake. Please take it all with a grain of salt.

# Want to Suggest Changes or Additions?

Make a [pull request](https://github.com/Purgenta889/SoftwareLoveTracker/pulls)! Issues are closed due to the potential risk of harassment this repository could receive if they were left open.

## Rules for Pull Requests

Follow these rules when making a pull request, otherwise you may not be accepted:

1. Follow the alphabetical order of all these lists
2. Use accurate links to your entry
3. Do not add terminal punctuation (periods, exclamation marks, etc.)
4. Remain formal and neutral in all your phrasing, regardless of the topic
5. Please be as concise as possible
6. Do not use entries to encourage hate or harassment towards anyone
7. Be descriptive in what you changed, added, and/or removed with your pull request

## Which Software and Organizations are Allowed?

### Software

The following are examples of software permitted on this list:

- Applications

- Operating systems and their components

- Modifications for applications or video games

- Hardware that requires software to function (i.e., a smartphone)

- Security software, such as anti-viruses and firewalls

- APIs

- Video game launchers, such as those for Minecraft

- Video game platforms, such as Steam

- Terminal-exclusive software, such as Neofetch

- Package managers

- Social media platforms

- E-mail platforms

- Coding languages

- Coding platforms

The following are examples **not** permitted on this list:

- News outlets

- Lists on Github or similar platforms, such as this very one

- Extremely niche software

- Unmaintained software

- Software that causes potential harm to a computer or user (Note: This includes both malicious software and software that unintentionally does so, due to a lack of maintenance)

- Rudimentary scripts

- Software developed and provided by governments

- Software exclusively functional on specific devices/hardware, such as the NVIDIA Control Panel

### Organizations

The following are examples of organizations permitted on this list:

- Non-profits or for-profits that involve at least one of the following:
  
  - Software R&D and maintenance
  
  - Operating system R&D and maintenance
  
  - Desktop environment R&D and maintenance
  
  - Social media R&D and maintenance
  
  - Coding platform R&D and maintenance
  
  - Coding language R&D and maintenance
  
  - Forum R&D and maintenance

The following are examples of organizations **not** permitted on this list:

- Non-profits or for-profits that involve at least one of the following:
  
  - Exclusively focusing on hardware production, such as Intel
  
  - Government services
  
  - Relief services
  
  - PACs and Super PACs of the United States
  
  - Corporations that provide necessary public services, such as medication and public transportation
  
  - Corporations that are not categorized officially as a "technology" corporation
  
  - Non-profits that are not categorized officially as a "technology" non-profit
  
  - Retail corporations
  
  - Energy corporations
  
  - Food/drink corporations

- Essentially, if the organization isn't explicitly involved in the field of software and its development, it can't be added here
